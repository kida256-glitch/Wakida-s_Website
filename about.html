About Me

I'm Benjamin Wakida Eldon, a Business Computing student at Makerere University Business School with a passion for connecting business strategy with cutting-edge technology. My journey in tech is driven by a simple belief: technology becomes truly powerful when it empowers communities.

As a student leader and tech enthusiast, I've dedicated myself to building bridges between students and emerging technologies. Whether it's organizing AWS workshops, leading blockchain discussions, or helping peers discover the power of automation tools, I thrive on creating spaces where learning happens naturally and innovation flourishes.

My work spans across cloud computing with AWS, Web3 and blockchain development, automation with UiPath, and AI-powered tools. But beyond the technical skills, what drives me is the opportunity to organize events, mentor fellow students, and build communities that last beyond the classroom.

From Uganda to the global tech community, I'm constantly exploring new ways to make technology accessible, practical, and impactful for students who are ready to shape the future.
