## 🚀 Quick Start (3 Steps)

### Step 1: View Your Website
1. Double-click `index.html`
2. It will open in your default browser
3. Explore all sections!

### Step 2: Add Your Photo (5 minutes)
1. Get a professional photo (square format, 400x400px recommended)
2. Name it `profile.jpg` and put it in the same folder as index.html
3. Open `index.html` in a text editor (Notepad, VS Code, etc.)
4. Find line ~65 (search for "image-placeholder")
5. Replace the entire `<div class="image-placeholder">` section with:
```html
<div class="image-placeholder">
    <img src="profile.jpg" alt="Benjamin Wakida" 
         style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; border-radius: 20px;">
</div>
```
6. Save and refresh your browser

### Step 3: Update Contact Email (2 minutes)
1. Open `script.js` in a text editor
2. Find line ~70 (search for "mailto:contact@example.com")
3. Replace `contact@example.com` with your actual email
4. Save and refresh your browser

**That's it! Your portfolio is now personalized!**
